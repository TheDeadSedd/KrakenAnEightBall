\# Prototype Notes



Gameplay loop:

\- Shoot cue ball

\- Sink balls for points

\- Trick shots give bonus score

\- Large trick shots spawn new balls

\- New balls can be special

\- Cue ball sunk = game over

\- 8 ball sunk = game over



Potential trick shot bonuses:

\- Bank shot

\- Combo shot

\- Multi-ball sink

\- Long chain collision

\- Rail ricochet

\- Fast shot

\- Last-second sink



Potential special balls:

\- Powder keg ball

\- Ghost ball

\- Heavy ball

\- Split ball

\- Kraken ball



Theme:

\- Pirate tavern billiards

\- Stormy ocean mood

\- Kraken influence

\- Slight eldritch atmosphere

\- Not full horror
