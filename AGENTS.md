\# Kraken An Eight Ball



A small pirate/kraken themed billiards roguelike prototype made in Godot 4.



The purpose of this prototype is to quickly test whether:

\- billiards + roguelike escalation is fun

\- trick-shot scoring feels satisfying

\- special balls create chaos and replayability

\- the theme has public appeal



The scope should remain intentionally small and polished.



Current prototype goals:

\- 2D top-down billiards gameplay

\- drag-to-shoot controls

\- realistic-enough arcade billiards physics

\- score system based on trick shots

\- spawning new balls after high-value shots

\- occasional special balls

\- pirate/kraken atmosphere



Important constraints:

\- Keep systems simple

\- Avoid overengineering

\- Prioritize "playable quickly"

\- Prefer readable beginner-friendly code

\- Keep scripts modular and small with no single function exceeding 60 lines

\- Explain major changes clearly in comments



Target platforms:

\- Windows first

\- Android later



Tech:

\- Godot 4

\- GDScript
